Dewan Ahmed is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) at [Harness](https://www.harness.io/). Since June 2026, he has been working with enterprise customers to build developer workflows, ticket triage automation, and other agent-driven processes, most recently with Cosmos Software Factory.

Before moving into forward deployed engineering, he was a Principal Developer Advocate at Harness. Before joining Harness, he worked at IBM, Red Hat, and Aiven as a developer, QA lead, consultant, and developer advocate. For the last twelve years, Dewan has worked to solve DevOps and infrastructure problems for small startups, large enterprises, and governments. Starting his public speaking at a toastmaster in 2016, he has been speaking at tech conferences and meetups for the last ten years.

He also builds small, vendor-neutral open-source tools for AI agents and software factories (see [Projects](/projects/)). On this blog he writes about building with agents, the protocols and sandboxes around them, developer advocacy, and his thoughts on a career in tech. His work is fueled by a passion for open source and a deep respect for the tech community.

Outside of work, he’s an advocate for underrepresented groups in tech and offers pro bono career coaching as his way of giving back.
